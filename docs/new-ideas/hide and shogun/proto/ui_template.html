Hide and Shogun

Thirty face-down tiles, two clans, one Shogun each. Unveil, march, strike, and topple the enemy Shogun before yours falls. This build plays against a bot so the rules can be tested.

Rule Reference

The turn

  • One Command: Unveil a Shroud, March one square, Strike, or Charge (Samurai).
  • The first coloured Unveil raises the banners and sets the colours.
  • A Geisha goes to whoever unveils her. A clan can hold at most half of them; a Geisha unveiled at the cap joins the opponent. Before the banners are raised she waits and takes the flipper's colour.
  • A Shogun is Guarded while any of his own Daimyo lives, face-down ones included.

The clocks (fixed rules)

  • Shogun's Shame: 5 of your own Commands with no Unveil and no capture while your Shogun is still face-down, and he is shamed out into the open. From then on only a face-up Daimyo guards him. A Shogun already face-up is never forced.
  • The Long Night: 12 quiet Commands in a row (6 each) ends the Battle. More face-up pieces wins; level is a Truce. Hired Geisha count for their clan.
  • No legal Command: you are Surrounded and lose.

The Campaign

  • Win a Battle, earn an Honour Seal. First to 3 Seals (or 5, or a single Battle) takes the Campaign.
  • A Truce earns no Seal and the Battle is replayed.
  • The Omen decides who commands first in Battle 1; after that the Vanguard alternates each Battle.

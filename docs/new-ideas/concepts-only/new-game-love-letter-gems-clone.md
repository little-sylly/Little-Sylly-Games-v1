How does love letter board play Love Letter is a brilliant exercise in minimalist game design. The entire game consists of just 16 cards (or 21 in newer editions). It blends risk, deduction, and luck into quick, hightension rounds that take about five minutes each. The thematic goal is simple: you are trying to get your love letter delivered to the Princess while intercepting the letters of your rivals. Love Letter Components. Source: Meeple Mountain Core Gameplay Loop The golden rule of Love Letter is incredibly straightforward: Honstly, you only ever do one of two things on your turn.1. 2. Draw: You start your turn with one card in your hand. You draw a second card from the deck. Play: You choose one of those two cards, place it face-up in front of you, and execute its text effect. That is it. You hold one, you play one, and then the turn passes clockwise. The Setup Shuffle the cards. The "Burn" Card: Remove the top card of the deck from the game face-down without anyone looking. (This creates hidden information so players can’t perfectly card-count at the end of the round). If playing a 2-player game, flip three more cards face-up next to the deck. Deal one card face-down to each player. How a Round Ends A round finishes in one of two ways: Knockout: If you are knocked out by a card effect, you discard your hand face-up and sit out until the next round. If only one player remains standing, they instantly win the round and get a Token of Affection (a point). Deck Depletion: If the draw deck runs out, everyone still alive reveals their single hand card. The player holding the card with the highest value wins the round. The first player to collect a set number of tokens (usually 3 to 5 depending on player count) wins the entire game. The Cards & Core Logic Every card has a point value (0 to 9) and a specific action.

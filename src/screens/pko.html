The Conditions 🌿

Set the terms of the territory.

Law of the Wild

What it takes to end up on top, and how long the Match runs. Dominance is a race to win Clashes. Stragglers plays a set number instead and counts whatever you are still holding each time somebody empties — fewest at the end takes it.

First to 3 Clashes takes the Match.

Hoard Size

How many cards each player is dealt at the start of every Clash. Bigger Hoards mean longer Clashes.

Appetite

How far down the chain a predator will reach. Ravenous means a Leopard also takes Mice, not just Mongooses — fewer Encounters go Unchallenged, which suits smaller tables. Sated is the strict chain: you only ever eat your direct prey.

Poacher Cards

How many Human wildcards go into the Pool. A Poacher wins any single Mark outright — and nothing else. One Each keeps pace with bigger tables.

Scavenge on Retreat

Draw a card when you Retreat. More options for later — but this is a race to empty your Hoard, so it's one more card to shed.

Small Fry

The player who opens must Stake their smallest animal. How many is still their call. Bees, Eagles and Stingrays sit outside the size ladder, so they never count as your smallest.

How to Play 🐘

Shed your whole hand by playing each animal's natural predator.

Step 1

The food chain

Every animal has a natural predator, and only its immediate predator counts. Eagles don't answer to Elephants.

Step 2

Staking your claim

Open an Encounter by Staking — put down one or more cards of the same species. Each one becomes a Mark the next player has to answer.

Small Fry

Open at the bottom

If Small Fry is on, whoever opens has to Stake their smallest animal — Mouse and Fish are the bottom of their chains. You still choose how many. Bees, Eagles, Stingrays and Poachers sit outside the ladder and never count as your smallest.

Step 3

The Challenge

Beat every Mark — one predator per Mark, or Swarm it. Miss one and you can't Challenge at all — it's all or nothing.

Step 4

Swarm it instead

No predator on hand? Play two cards of the Mark's own species and Swarm it. Each becomes a Mark of its own, so the board grows one wider. A Poacher-Mark can't be Swarmed.

Step 5

Stampede

When every Mark is the same species, play one more copy of that species than there are Marks and the whole field is yours. The button appears on its own when you hold enough.

Step 6

Retreat and rejoin

Can't beat everything? Retreat — you're not out. The moment the Marks change you're back in and can Challenge again.

Step 7

Going Unchallenged

When everyone else has Retreated you go Unchallenged, win the Encounter, and lead the next one.

The Poacher

Answers to no one

The Human wildcard wins any single Mark outright — no predator relationship needed. That's the whole of it: it can't stand in as an animal, it can't help you Stampede, and it can neither Swarm nor be Swarmed. There are only a handful in the Pool.

The Eagle

Nothing eats an Eagle

If there's an Eagle among the Marks, only a Poacher or a Swarm of two Eagles can answer it — unless the whole field is Eagles and you can Stampede it.

Winning and Scoring

  • Empty your Hoard — play your last card and you take the Clash. The moment you do, that Clash is over for everyone.
  • Dominance — every Clash you take is worth a point, and the first to the target takes the Match.
  • Stragglers — a set number of Clashes instead. Every card still in your Hoard when a Clash ends is a Straggler against you, and the fewest at the end takes the Match.
  • The Hierarchy ranks everyone at the end — Apex Predator down to the Bottom Feeder.

✨ Sylly Mode

Force of Nature

Every Clash opens with Invasive Mimicry — Mimics join the Pool and everyone draws a few extra. After that, each Encounter opens on one of eight events: The Culling, The Great Reversal, The Deluge, The Dry Season, Extinction Event, Migration, Alpha or Carrion. A Mimic copies whatever it's played with, so it always needs a real animal beside it — and it can never copy a Poacher.

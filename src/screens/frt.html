✨ Sylly Mode

Fruity Personalities

Every fruit variety gains a unique, rule-breaking quirk. Requires 3+ players.

Fruity Personalities is unavailable while Pear-Off is on.

How to Play 🍌

Bluff your fruit. Don't get caught holding four.

Step 1

The Serve

On your turn, slide one card from your Fruit Stash face-down to another player and declare a fruit — the truth, or a bald-faced lie. 🍌

Step 2

The Stare Down

The receiver judges you. They Call True, Call False, or Peek & Pass — sneak a look, then slide it onward to someone who hasn’t handled it yet, with a fresh claim.

Step 3

Getting Caught

When someone calls, the card flips. Guess wrong, or get caught in a lie, and the card lands face-up in your Fruit Bowl. Whoever just collected it serves next.

Step 4

Fruit Looped

Collect 4 of the same fruit in your bowl (5 in a 2-player duel), or get stuck with no cards to serve, and you’re Fruit Looped — the round ends.

Winning and Scoring

Fruit Tokens across the Fruit-Offs

  • Fruit Looped — 0 tokens that round.
  • Survived the Salad — +5 tokens.
  • Pristine Clean Escape — +10 if your bowl is empty.
  • The Silver Lining — +2 for the most correct calls all game (“Fruit Master” ✨).

Fruity Personalities 🍓

Every fruit's rule-breaking quirk in Sylly Mode.

🍌 Smug Banana on-challenge

When an opponent loses a challenge against a Banana, they take the card as normal — but you keep the serve.

🍋 Sour Lemon on-challenge

Whenever a Lemon is revealed, the other party flips one random card from their own Stash — win or lose, nobody walks away clean.

🍑 Charming Peach on-challenge

If a Peach lands in a Bowl, the challenger must flip one of their own Peaches from their Stash — if they're holding one.

🍇 Dramatic Grape on-challenge

When a Grape is flipped, whoever is hiding the most Grapes must flip one into their Bowl. Ties? Everyone tied flips one.

🍉 Chill Watermelon passive

A live counter shows the whole table how many Watermelons each player is hiding. No secrets.

🍐 Sus Pear on-peek

If you Peek and find a Pear, you can pocket it and swap any card from your Stash to pass on instead, with a fresh claim.

🍓 Panicked Strawberry on-serve

Every time you serve a Strawberry, there's a 1-in-4 chance it panics and auto-reveals into your own Bowl. No bluffing required.

🍎 Angry Apple on-challenge

When an Apple is flipped, the loser must serve the winner on their next turn — and the winner cannot Peek.

✨ Sylly Mode

Night Terrors

Reach 99 and the dream inverts: the count plunges, every number flips its sign, and the ceiling falls each turn. Knocked-out players linger as Sleepwalkers and can nudge the dream through the Nightmare Meter. Last one awake survives.

How to Play 🐑

Keep the herd below 99. Don't be the one who nods off.

Step 1

The Count

On your turn, play one card to change the Herd. Number cards add sheep - but if your play would push the count past 99, it's illegal.

Step 2

Pillows & Alarms

Pillows stall or shrink the count (Doze, Counting Backwards, Lullaby, Swap Dreams). Alarms shake it up — Skip a Few, the Black Sheep (snaps to 99), Wide Awake, Heavy Eyelids and Rude Awakening. The Big Bad Wolf hides in the Flock — draw one and a card slot locks for the Night. Not sure what a card does? Press and hold it in your hand, or check The Cards tab above.

Step 3

Dozing Off

Bust the count, or run out of safe cards, and you doze off - you're out for the night. Everyone else plays on from exactly where the Herd sits.

Winning and Scoring

Last one awake

  • Last One Awake - win the Night and earn a Moon 🌙.
  • Daybreak - first to the target number of Moons wins the match. Set it in Settings.
  • A busted play is rolled back - the Herd returns to where it was before your card, so the Night carries on unchanged without you.

✨ Sylly Mode

Night Terrors

One long Night - no redeals, and your Moons are lives. Crash and you lose one, then the Jolt wakes you with a fresh hand. Reach 99 and the dream inverts - the Plunge: every number card flips its sign and the ceiling falls a little each turn. At zero Moons you don't just leave - you become a Sleepwalker, haunting the dream. Every Pasture card played charges the Nightmare Meter; when it fills, a Sleepwalker flips one of three face-down nightmares onto the living. 💤 Last one awake survives the night.

How to Play 💻

Build a maze so the breach signal takes the longest possible path.

Step 1

Harden the Node

Place Firewall Segments and Honeypots to force the Breach Vector along the longest route from Ingress to Egress. You can't seal the exit off — those placements are blocked automatically.

Controls

Tap and long-press

  • Tap empty cell — place a Firewall Segment.
  • Long-press empty cell — place a Honeypot (slow trap).
  • Long-press a Firewall — upgrade it to a Honeypot.
  • Tap a block — remove it.

Symbol Legend

  • ▶ Ingress / Egress Port
  • ▩ Bad Sector (fixed obstacle)
  • ▓ Firewall Segment (your wall)
  • ❄ Honeypot (slow trap)
  • · Null Sector (open)

Winning and Scoring

System Efficiency Rating

  • Longest delay each cycle scores 100.00%; everyone else is scored relative to it.
  • Corners earn you time — every turn the signal makes adds delay, and delay is score.
  • Highest rolling-average SER across all cycles wins.

Debug Mode

Staging Environment

One analyst authors the Node by hand in the Node Editor — drawing Bad Sectors, dropping Native Honeypots, placing the ports and setting everyone's resources. Once it is deployed, you can re-run your trace as many times as you like; only your best attempt is scored. Not available alongside Sylly Mode.

✨ Sylly Mode

Distributed Network Protocol

Two corporate clusters pool resources and chain their Nodes into one continuous relay. A Lead Systems Engineer allocates the team's inventory; the signal carries its speed and slow state across each Bridge.

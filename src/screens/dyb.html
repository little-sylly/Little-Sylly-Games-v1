How to Play 🎲

A bluffing game where every claim must be worth believing.

Step 1

The Shake

Everyone taps to roll their dice simultaneously. Your dice are private — nobody else sees them until The Overlook.

Step 2

The Claim

The opener makes a claim: "At least X dice showing face Y exist across the whole table." This becomes The Claim.

Step 3

Climb or Challenge

Each player must either climb higher or tap Call the Bluff to trigger The Overlook. Quantity can never decrease.

Winning and Scoring

Last Die Standing

  • Bluff called correctly — the bidder loses a die.
  • Bluff called wrong — the challenger loses a die.
  • Out of dice — you're eliminated. Watch from The Depths.
  • Last player with dice — reaches The Summit.

✨ Sylly Mode

The Tempest

Some dice are secretly cursed. Loaded count double, Phantom hide their face until The Overlook, Slick let you assign any face, Cracked count zero, and Snake Eyes subtract from the tally.

Cookie Playbook 🍪

How the raid runs — and how much trouble you're in.

Snack Friendly

Guarantees the first card or two of every Raid is cookies, so nobody gets busted before they've grabbed anything.

House Rules

What happens to a family member after they catch you. Standard Burn makes them a little less likely next time; High Alert makes someone more likely.

Match Length

How many Cookie Raids before the biggest Cookie Stash wins.

Decision Time

How long everyone gets to choose each flip. No Rush turns the clock off completely — the jar waits for the slowest hand.

Open Book

Shows everyone's cookies to everyone. Turn it off and you'll only see your own numbers — you'll hear what happened to the others, just not how much.

How to Play 🍪

Grab cookies, don't get caught twice.

Step 1

A card comes out of the jar

Everyone sees it at once. It's either cookies 🍪, a member of the family 👪, or a Treat 🍰 that lands on the counter.

Step 2

Cookies get shared out straight away

Split evenly between everyone still raiding. Anything left over that won't divide becomes Cookie Crumbs, sitting on the bench.

Step 3

Then everyone decides, at the same time

Reach In Again to stay in for the next card, or Sneak Out to bank everything you've collected this Raid. Decision Time is a setting — Blitz, Standard, or No Rush with no clock at all.

Step 4

Sneak out alone and you take the crumbs

The only one leaving? You scoop every crumb on the bench and any Treat on the counter. Leave with someone else and you just split the crumbs.

Step 5

The family is watching

The first time someone turns up, it's just a scare. The second time the same person turns up, it's BUSTED! — everyone still in the kitchen loses the lot.

Step 6

Banked cookies are safe forever

A bust only costs you what you were carrying this Raid. Your Cookie Stash can never go down.

Step 7

Five Raids, one jar

Each Raid starts a fresh jar. Check the Crumb Trail 🔍 any time to see every flip and who's still lurking in the deck.

Winning and Scoring

Biggest Cookie Stash takes it

  • Most cookies banked — Top Cookie Thief 🍪.
  • Tied? Whoever grabbed more Treats wins it.
  • Still tied? You share the spot.
  • Last place gets caught Red-Handed.

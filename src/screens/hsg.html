How to Play 🏯

Two clans, thirty doors, a Shogun hiding behind one of them.

Step 1

Every piece hides behind a door

All thirty start closed. Anyone may open any door.

Step 2

One Command a turn

On your go, open a door, march a piece of yours, or strike. Tap a piece to see where it can go and what it can hit.

Step 3

Raising the Banners

The first clan piece anyone finds decides who is Crimson and who is Indigo.

Step 4

The ladder

Higher strikes lower, up close: Shogun, Guard, Monk, Samurai, Ninja, Archer, Farmer.

Step 5

The specialists

  • Archer — hits 2 or 3 squares away.
  • Ninja — Leaps over one piece.
  • Monk — moves on the diagonal.
  • Samurai — Charges 2.
  • Farmer — lowest rank, but chases off foxes.

Easy to miss: the Archer and the Ninja can't hit an adjacent piece.

Step 6

The Guard

While a Guard stands — even one still behind a door — the Shogun is Guarded. When both Guards fall, he is Unguarded.

Step 7

The foxes

Find a fox and she sides with you, up to your share. She's the only piece that can reach a Guarded Shogun, and only a Farmer or the Ninja can stop her.

Easy to miss: your third fox goes to your opponent.

Step 8

The Last Lantern

Twelve quiet Commands in a row and the Battle ends: whoever has the most pieces in the open wins.

Easy to miss: closed doors don't count.

Winning and Scoring

Seals take the Campaign

  • Strike their Shogun, or lead when the last lantern goes out, to win the Battle.
  • One Seal per Battle — first to 3 takes the Campaign (the War Council can change it).
  • A Truce is replayed. The first move alternates every Battle.

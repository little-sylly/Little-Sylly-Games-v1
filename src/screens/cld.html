How to Play 🐧

Shove your mates off the ice. Last one dry wins.

Step 1

You're one penguin on a crowded floe

Everyone gets shoved off eventually. Be the last one who isn't.

Step 2

Drag back to aim, like a slingshot

Pull back from your own penguin — further back is harder. Release to arm it, then tap Lock It In to commit. Re-drag as many times as you like before that, but once you lock in, that's your Slide.

Step 3

Everyone aims at the same time

Nobody sees anyone else's plan until every player has locked in. You're not reacting to a rival's move — you're guessing it.

Step 4

All penguins slide at once

You will hit people you didn't aim at. That's the game working correctly, not a bug.

Step 5

Off the edge and you're in the Drink

Drowned for the rest of this Floe-Off — but not benched.

Step 6

Being Drowned doesn't bench you

You bob at a Berth on the rim and do two things every Slide: you're a bumper, and you get a Snowball to nudge someone's aim off — stronger the closer you are, which is why you can Dive one Berth left or right to chase the action.

Easy to miss: a Drowned penguin does not save whoever hits them — it shoves them back harder, which is usually worse. Also easy to miss: a Dive picks a Berth, not a spot — where exactly you surface is up to the sea.

Ice Breaker

The floe starts guarded

Chunks of ice near the edge bounce you back instead of dumping you in, until they've taken enough hits and shatter. Handy early. Don't count on it forever.

Peck Off

You've got two penguins

Aim both, commit both. You're only out when both are in the Drink — and a Drowned penguin of yours still throws Snowballs while the other one skates.

Winning and Scoring

Last one dry catches a Fish

  • Last player Standing — catches a 🐟. First to the Fish target wins the whole thing.
  • Shoving someone in — no Fish. Nobody's rewarded for it, so nobody can farm it.
  • Washout — nobody left after a Slide. No Fish, everyone Resurfaces, run it again.

✨ Sylly Mode

The Thaw

The floe melts a little every Slide, shrinking until there's nowhere left to stand. The Drowned close in with the rim — and if the ice runs out from under you, you're going in whether you moved or not.

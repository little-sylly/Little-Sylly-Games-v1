How to Play 📋

A social deduction game of trust, betrayal, and terrible excuses.

Step 1

Everyone gets a secret role

Players are split into Friends and Flakes. Friends want the plans to succeed. Flakes want them to fail — without getting caught.

Step 2

Picking the group

The Planner nominates a group to carry out the plan. Everyone votes: Sounds Good or No Way. Majority rules. If a group is rejected 5 times in a row, the Flakes win immediately.

Step 3

The mission cards

Once the group is approved, each member privately submits a card. Friends must submit I'm In. Flakes can choose to bail — anonymously. Even one bail causes the plan to fail.

Step 4

Five plans, pass the phone

The game runs for up to 5 plans. Group sizes grow as the game progresses.

Winning and Scoring

First to three wins

  • Friends — win when 3 plans succeed.
  • Flakes — win when 3 plans fail.
  • 5 rejections in a row — Flakes win instantly, no mission needed.

✨ Sylly Mode

Drama Mode

One Friend secretly knows all the Flakes — The Pot-Stirrer. One Flake becomes The Big Flake. If the Friends win, the Big Flake gets one shot to name The Pot-Stirrer. A correct guess steals the win.

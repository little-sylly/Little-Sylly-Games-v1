        How to Play 🛵

        Guess how long an odd vehicle takes, then argue for it.

        Step 1

        Clock on

        You're a courier at E.T.A. Each Run, Dotty hands out a job docket — the Route and its Dispatch Briefing — and you're secretly given a Ride. Nobody tells you its speed.

        Step 2

        Log your ETA

        Estimate how long your Ride would take, pick a unit on the Time Dial, and lock it in. Dotty's Double-Take repeats it in plain words to catch slips. Constant speed, no stops — unless the Briefing says otherwise.

        Step 3

        The Run Sheet

        Everyone's ETAs fill in on one sheet, quickest at the left of the scale. Each Ride's mode badge shows how it travels — Land, Sea, Air or Space.

        Step 4

        Talk Me Through It

        Defend your number out loud. Then give one Stamp of Approval to the best pitch, not the best guess — funny or convincing, your call. Each Stamp pays that courier a Depot Tip. You can't stamp yourself, and you can change your Stamp until everyone's in.

        Step 5

        Proof of Delivery

        A parcel drops on the true ETA for every Ride and its real speed is revealed. The three closest are stamped 1st, 2nd and 3rd, and closer means more Mileage. It's judged by how many times out you were, not the gap — 2× off on a 5-minute trip scores the same as 2× off on 10 years.

        Step 6

        The Odometer

        Mileage spins up and you can be promoted. Ranks only last for this Shift.

        Winning and Scoring

        Most Mileage is Courier of the Month

        • Accuracy pays the most — up to 40,000 km a Run, full marks within 5%.
        • Depot Tips — every Stamp you receive pays a little.
        • Tied? You share the title.

        ✨ Sylly Mode

        Backseat Drivers

        Instead of Stamps, grab one other courier's wheel and make one secret Course Correction to their ETA. Fixing a big miss pays big, but corrections multiply, so a pile-on can overshoot into a Spin-out. Unregistered Rides (no rego!) join the shed and pay Hazard Pay. Corrections never change the target's own accuracy score.

        The rank ladder

        Mileage this Shift sets your rank

          Every Shift Length uses the same ladder. Mileage keeps counting after the top rank — the Odometer decides the winner.

          How Mileage is earned

          Accuracy, plus a little for the pitch

          Accuracy: 40,000 km within 5% of the truth, tapering to nothing at about 18× off (2× off ≈ 27,000). Depot Tips: each Stamp you receive, a little less at bigger tables. Sweep Bonus: stamped by everyone else, at 5 or more couriers.

          ✨ Backseat Drivers

          How corrections pay

          • Corrections multiply: ×2 and ×2 make ×4.
          • Pushed the ETA closer? Share the Navigation Assist. Pushed it worse? Pay a Detour Penalty.
          • A pile-on that ends further off is a Spin-out; one that lands close is Back on Course.
          • Being corrected pays an Insurance Payout. Hands off is always allowed.
          • Hazard Pay: 4,000 km for holding an Unregistered Ride.

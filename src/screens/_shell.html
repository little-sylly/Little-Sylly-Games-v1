The Workshop 🎮

Make the controller yours.

Step 1

Test the buttons

Try having fun with the buttons — you might just find a secret.

Step 2

Pick your colours

Four parts, twenty colours — one for every game in the box. Choose a part, then tap a swatch, and the controller changes straight away.

Step 3

Save it, or start again

Save keeps your design for next time. Reset puts it back to the colours it came in. Leaving without saving changes nothing.

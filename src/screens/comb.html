How to Play 🐝

Gather from the meadow, trade with the table, build the biggest comb.

Step 1

Grow the strongest hive

First colony to the Hive Point target wins — and the win is only ever checked on your own turn.

Step 2

The meadow is nineteen hexes

Every hex makes one resource and carries a number. Corners are where you build cells; edges are where you build walls.

Step 3

The Scout Flight decides what blooms

One cast per turn, and it pays everyone at once — not just the active player. Easy to miss: you collect on other people's turns too, which is why you watch every roll.

Step 4

Cells collect, Domes collect double

A Drone Cell touching a blooming hex gives you 1 of its resource. A Queen Dome gives 2.

Step 5

Four things to spend on

Comb Wall, Drone Cell, Queen Dome and an Instinct card. The full prices are in The Comb tab — and in placement mode only what you can afford lights up.

Step 6

The Distance Rule

A new Drone Cell must sit at least two edges from any other cell — including your own. It is the single most-missed rule in the game.

Step 7

Everything must connect

Whatever you build has to touch your own network. The exception is your two opening placements, which are free and go anywhere legal.

Step 8

The Waggle Dance

Trade with the table on any terms you can talk someone into, or with the Meadow at 4:1. Landing a Drone Cell on a Trade Blossom makes the Meadow cheaper.

Step 9

A 7 brings the Wasp

Nothing blooms, over-stuffed players drop half, and the roller parks the Wasp somewhere painful. Easy to miss: the discard hits everyone over the limit, not just the roller's target.

Step 10

One Instinct card a turn

And never on the turn you bought it. The exception is Golden Nectar, which is never played at all — it just counts at the end.

Step 11

Largest Comb and Fiercest Guard

Two points each, and they can be taken off you. Easy to miss: someone placing a cell in the middle of your walls cuts your chain.

Winning and Scoring

Where the Hive Points come from

  • Drone Cell — 1 point each.
  • Queen Dome — 2 points each.
  • Largest Comb — 2 points, to the longest unbroken chain of walls. Ties never transfer it.
  • Fiercest Guard — 2 points, to whoever has played the most Guard Bees. Ties never transfer it.
  • Golden Nectar — 1 point each, hidden in your hand until somebody wins.

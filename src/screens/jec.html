How to Play 🍳

Think the same as just enough people — not too many, not too few.

Step 1

Everyone sees the same Order.

A dish — like "Pizza" or "Nachos" — appears on screen. With Special Instructions on, it comes with a twist: on a $5 budget, or at 3am.

Step 2

Each Chef secretly picks 3 ingredients.

What would you put in this dish? You want to match others — without being so obvious that everybody does.

Step 3

Back one with a star.

Tap the ⭐ to make one ingredient your Signature Dish. If it lands in the golden range, it scores double. If it doesn't, it just scores what it was worth — the only loss is the double you missed.

Step 4

Call the Crutch.

Every kitchen has one. Name the culinary cliché you reckon everyone leans on — it can't be one of your own three. Get the round's most-picked ingredient, shared by 3 or more Chefs, and you've Called It! 📣 Miss and it costs you nothing.

Step 5

Sous Chef's Check, then The Tasting.

First the table merges any ingredient that's the same word spelt two ways — before anyone can see what it's worth. Then The Tasting reveals the counts, quietest first.

Winning and Scoring

Most points after the last course wins.

  • 🍽️ Table for One — nobody else picked it. No points (with the penalty on: −5 pts).
  • ✨ Chef's Kiss — exactly one other Chef picked it. The jackpot.
  • 👌 Crowd-Pleaser — a few of you picked it. Half the jackpot.
  • 🍲 Too Many Cooks — everybody picked it. A token (with the tax on: −2 pts per Chef who picked it, including you).
  • 🌟 Signature Dish — a golden nomination scores double.
  • 📣 Called It! — a correct Crutch pays half the jackpot.

✨ Sylly Mode

Fusion Cuisine

Two Orders drop at once — Sushi + Pizza. Prep three ingredients for the mash-up and give the thing a name, all in one go. After The Tasting everyone votes on the best name; the winner goes On the Menu! ⭐ for half the jackpot. Ties all pay.

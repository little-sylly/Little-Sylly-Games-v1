      How to Play 📡

      Encrypt your vault. Intercept theirs.

      Step 1

      Each team memorises their vault.

      Your team gets 4 secret keywords numbered 1–4. Only your team can see them — memorise them!

      Step 2

      Encrypt your broadcast.

      Each round, your Encoder receives a secret code (e.g. 3-1-4). Give one clue per number — cryptic enough to fool the enemy, clear enough for your own team.

      Step 3

      Intercept or decode.

      The enemy team tries to crack your code first — that's an 🕵️ Interception. Then your own team decodes it. Get it wrong and it's a Misfire 💥.

      Winning and Scoring

      Intercept to win. Don't misfire.

      • 🕵️ Intercept — enemy cracked your code. They score.
      • 💥 Misfire — your own team got it wrong. Two misfires and you lose.
      • First team to reach the interception target wins.

      How to Play 🧠

      Find the same connecting word as your partner.

      Step 1

      Two players. One word pair.

      You're each shown the same pair of words. Your job: think of one word that connects them both — without talking to each other.

      Step 2

      Enter your word privately.

      No peeking! Each player enters their word without showing the other. You also can't use any part of either starting word.

      Step 3

      Reveal and check for a match.

      After the countdown, both answers are revealed. If they match — Mind Meld! 🧠 If not, your two words become the new starting pair. Keep going until you meld!

      Winning and Scoring

      Fewer rounds is more impressive.

      The game ends when you achieve Neural Link — both words match. The fewer rounds it took, the stronger the connection. Lower rounds = bigger brains. 🧠

      How to Play 🍳

      Think the same as just enough people — not too many, not too few.

      Step 1

      Everyone sees the same Order.

      A dish — like "Pizza" or "Nachos" — appears on screen. With Special Instructions on, it comes with a twist: on a $5 budget, or at 3am.

      Step 2

      Each Chef secretly picks 3 ingredients.

      What would you put in this dish? You want to match others — without being so obvious that everybody does.

      Step 3

      Back one with a star.

      Tap the ⭐ to make one ingredient your Signature Dish. If it lands in the golden range, it scores double. If it doesn't, it just scores what it was worth — the only loss is the double you missed.

      Step 4

      Call the Crutch.

      Every kitchen has one. Name the culinary cliché you reckon everyone leans on — it can't be one of your own three. Get the round's most-picked ingredient, shared by 3 or more Chefs, and you've Called It! 📣 Miss and it costs you nothing.

      Step 5

      Sous Chef's Check, then The Tasting.

      First the table merges any ingredient that's the same word spelt two ways — before anyone can see what it's worth. Then The Tasting reveals the counts, quietest first.

      Winning and Scoring

      Most points after the last course wins.

      • 🍽️ Table for One — nobody else picked it. No points (with the penalty on: −5 pts).
      • ✨ Chef's Kiss — exactly one other Chef picked it. The jackpot.
      • 👌 Crowd-Pleaser — a few of you picked it. Half the jackpot.
      • 🍲 Too Many Cooks — everybody picked it. A token (with the tax on: −2 pts per Chef who picked it, including you).
      • 🌟 Signature Dish — a golden nomination scores double.
      • 📣 Called It! — a correct Crutch pays half the jackpot.

      ✨ Sylly Mode

      How to Play 🏃‍♂️

      Figure out the secret address. Don't blow your cover.

      Step 1

      Roles are assigned in secret.

      One player is the Friend of a Friend — they don't know the location and are trying to blend in. Everyone else is The Gang — they see a shortlist of possible locations that narrows each plan.

      Step 2

      Players message each other over 4 Plans.

      Each lap, every player messages one other player. The Gang's shortlist narrows: 6 → 3 → 1 location. By Plan 3, The Gang knows the exact spot — but the Friend of a Friend might too if they've been paying attention.

      Survival of the Fittest

      Day 1 everyone shares the same broad clue. Day 2 and beyond, researchers reveal their specific detail while the Mole holds on.

      How to Play 🦁

      Natural Selection — a wildlife documentary goes wrong.

      Step 1

      Roles are Assigned.

      Each habitat draws a new Specimen. Roles are secretly assigned and revealed one at a time:

      • 🔬 Lead Biologist (1) — sees the full animal name.
      • 📋 Field Researchers — each sees a different detail word about the animal.
      • 🕵️ The Mole (1) — only knows the broad grouping (e.g. "Sea Creature"). Trying to blend in.

      Step 2

      The Observation.

      Over several Observation Days, each player submits one word to describe the Specimen. Turn order is random each day. You can't use the animal's name, and you can't repeat a word already given this habitat.

      Step 3

      The Selection.

      Once all days are done, the field notes are revealed. Each player votes for who they think is The Mole. Most votes = exposed.

      Step 4

      The Last Stand.

      If The Mole is caught, they get one final chance — name the Specimen. The Lead Biologist decides if it counts. +10 Credibility either way for a correct guess.

      Winning and Scoring

      Credibility decides the winner.

      • Mole escapes → +Escape Bonus to The Mole.
      • Mole names Specimen correctly → +10 to The Mole.
      • Mole caught → +10 to each Field Researcher & Biologist.
      • Discredited clue (Peer Review) → −5 to the author.

      ✨ Sylly Mode

      Survival of the Fittest

      No Lead Biologist. Every player gets a detail word — including the player normally in that role. All clues stay hidden until The Daily Review reveals them before voting.

      How to Play ⚓

      How to run a successful deployment.

      Step 1

      The Grid.

      A 5×5 grid of 25 words is dealt face-down. Words belong to one of four roles: Friendly Payloads (9 for the first team, 8 for the second), Enemy Payloads, Spiked Urchins, and Mines. Only the Captain can see the roles.

      Step 2

      The Captain gives a Sonar Ping.

      The Captain sees the colour-coded grid and gives the Crew a Sonar Ping — one word + one number. The word hints at multiple targets. The number says how many payloads it covers.

      Step 3

      The Crew taps their sequence.

      Using the Sonar Ping, the Crew taps grid words in the order they want to reveal them, then launches the sequence. You can guess up to the Captain's number + 1, or stop early.

      Step 4

      Hazard outcomes.

      • ✅ Friendly Payload — +10 Valour, keep going.
      • ⚠️ Enemy Payload — enemy gains +10 Valour.
      • 🦔 Spiked Urchin — −5 Valour.
      • 💣 Pressure Mine — −20 Valour.
      • 💀 Nuclear Mine — −1000 Valour, game over.

      Whether hazards end your turn is controlled in Settings.

      Winning and Scoring

      Highest Valour wins.

      The game ends when a team arms all of their payloads. The team with the highest Valour wins — not necessarily the team that finished first.

        The Abyss

        Every pass feeds a face-up central pool — The Abyss. Win a trick with a Detonation Combo (Triplet, Quad, Double Joker, Sequence or Double Sequence) and the Abyss detonates: its cards deal clockwise to everyone but the winner. Singles and Pairs stay quiet. Hit 13 cards and it fractures — everyone draws.

        How to Play 🃏

        A climbing card game — shed your hand faster than everyone else.

        Step 1

        Deal and sort

        Each player is dealt a hand of cards. Sort yours however you like — no one else can see them.

        Step 2

        Lowest card leads first

        Whoever holds the lowest card starts the very first round — that's the 3♦ (ties break by suit: ♦, ♣, ♥, ♠). Their opening combo must include that card. After round one, the winner of each round leads the next with anything.

        Step 3

        Play a combo or pass

        On your turn, play a combo (Single, Pair, Triplet, Quad, Sequence, or Double Sequence) that is exactly one rank higher than the table — or pass. A Bomb (Triplet+) beats any non-Bomb combo.

        Step 4

        Clear the table

        If everyone passes in a full circuit, the table clears and the last player to play leads fresh. 2s beat everything except a Bomb — but still follow the +1 climb rule.

        Step 5

        Empty your hand to win

        The first player to play their last card wins the round. The others lose chips based on how many cards remain.

        Winning and Scoring

        Chips decide the match

        • Still holding 13+ cards — lose 3× your card count
        • Never played a card — lose 2× your card count
        • Played at least once — lose 1× your card count
        • Winner — collects everyone else's penalties
        • Match ends when rounds run out or any player hits zero chips

        ✨ Sylly Mode

        The Abyss

        Every Pass feeds a card into a face-up central pool. Win a trick with a Detonation Combo (Triplet, Quad, Double Joker, Sequence or Double Sequence) and the pool detonates — its cards deal clockwise to everyone but the winner. Singles and Pairs keep the trick quiet, even on a winning play. A Detonation Combo that empties your hand distributes the pool before scoring. Reach 13 cards and the Abyss fractures — everyone draws one.

        How to Play 🐘

        Shed your whole hand by playing each animal's natural predator.

        Step 1

        The food chain

        Every animal has a natural predator, and only its immediate predator counts. Eagles don't answer to Elephants.

        Step 2

        Staking your claim

        Open an Encounter by Staking — put down one or more cards of the same species. Each one becomes a Mark the next player has to answer.

        Small Fry

        Open at the bottom

        If Small Fry is on, whoever opens has to Stake their smallest animal — Mouse and Fish are the bottom of their chains. You still choose how many. Bees, Eagles, Stingrays and Poachers sit outside the ladder and never count as your smallest.

        Step 3

        The Challenge

        Beat every Mark — one predator per Mark, or Swarm it. Miss one and you can't Challenge at all — it's all or nothing.

        Step 4

        Swarm it instead

        No predator on hand? Play two cards of the Mark's own species and Swarm it. Each becomes a Mark of its own, so the board grows one wider. A Poacher-Mark can't be Swarmed.

        Step 5

        Stampede

        When every Mark is the same species, play one more copy of that species than there are Marks and the whole field is yours. The button appears on its own when you hold enough.

        Step 6

        Retreat and rejoin

        Can't beat everything? Retreat — you're not out. The moment the Marks change you're back in and can Challenge again.

        Step 7

        Going Unchallenged

        When everyone else has Retreated you go Unchallenged, win the Encounter, and lead the next one.

        The Poacher

        Answers to no one

        The Human wildcard wins any single Mark outright — no predator relationship needed. That's the whole of it: it can't stand in as an animal, it can't help you Stampede, and it can neither Swarm nor be Swarmed. There are only a handful in the Pool.

        The Eagle

        Nothing eats an Eagle

        If there's an Eagle among the Marks, only a Poacher or a Swarm of two Eagles can answer it — unless the whole field is Eagles and you can Stampede it.

        Winning and Scoring

        • Empty your Hoard — play your last card and you take the Clash. The moment you do, that Clash is over for everyone.
        • Dominance — every Clash you take is worth a point, and the first to the target takes the Match.
        • Stragglers — a set number of Clashes instead. Every card still in your Hoard when a Clash ends is a Straggler against you, and the fewest at the end takes the Match.
        • The Hierarchy ranks everyone at the end — Apex Predator down to the Bottom Feeder.

        How to Play 🍪

        Grab cookies, don't get caught twice.

        Step 1

        A card comes out of the jar

        Everyone sees it at once. It's either cookies 🍪, a member of the family 👪, or a Treat 🍰 that lands on the counter.

        Step 2

        Cookies get shared out straight away

        Split evenly between everyone still raiding. Anything left over that won't divide becomes Cookie Crumbs, sitting on the bench.

        Step 3

        Then everyone decides, at the same time

        Reach In Again to stay in for the next card, or Sneak Out to bank everything you've collected this Raid. Decision Time is a setting — Blitz, Standard, or No Rush with no clock at all.

        Step 4

        Sneak out alone and you take the crumbs

        The only one leaving? You scoop every crumb on the bench and any Treat on the counter. Leave with someone else and you just split the crumbs.

        Step 5

        The family is watching

        The first time someone turns up, it's just a scare. The second time the same person turns up, it's BUSTED! — everyone still in the kitchen loses the lot.

        Step 6

        Banked cookies are safe forever

        A bust only costs you what you were carrying this Raid. Your Cookie Stash can never go down.

        Step 7

        Five Raids, one jar

        Each Raid starts a fresh jar. Check the Crumb Trail 🔍 any time to see every flip and who's still lurking in the deck.

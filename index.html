      How to Play

      Secret Signals

      Step 1 — The Vault 🔐

      Each team gets 4 secret keywords.

      Memorise them! They're numbered 1–4 and only your team can see them.

      Step 2 — Encrypt 📡

      Each round, one player gets a secret code.

      The code is 3 numbers (e.g. 3-1-4). Give one clue for each number — cryptic enough that the other team can't guess it, clear enough that your own team can!

      Step 3 — Intercept 🕵️

      The opposing team tries to crack your code.

      They see your clues and all previous clues — patterns emerge over time! If they guess correctly: they score an Interception.

      Step 4 — Decode 🔑

      Your own team must also decode the code.

      If your own team gets it wrong — that's a Misfire. You're talking in riddles no one understands!

      Winning 🏆

      Win by intercepting the enemy's transmissions.
      Lose by misfiring twice — your own team can't understand you!

      How to Play 🦁

      Natural Selection — a wildlife documentary goes wrong.

      The Setup

      Each habitat in the expedition focuses on a new Specimen. Roles are secretly assigned at the start of each habitat:

      • 🔬 Lead Biologist (1) — sees the full animal name.
      • 📋 Field Researchers — each sees a different detail word about the animal.
      • 🕵️ The Mole (1) — only knows the broad grouping (e.g. "Sea Creature"). Trying to blend in.

      The Observation

      Over several days, each player submits exactly one word to describe the specimen. Turn order is random each day. You can't use the animal's name, and you can't repeat any Field Observation already given this habitat.

      The Selection

      Once all observation days are done, the field notes are revealed. Each player makes their Classification — privately identifying who they think is The Mole. The most-voted player is exposed.

      Final Identification

      After each Habitat, The Mole makes their Final Identification — naming the specimen regardless of whether they were caught. The Lead Biologist decides if it counts.

      Scoring

      • Mole escapes undetected → +Escape Bonus Credibility to the Mole (configurable in Settings, default 10).
      • Mole correctly names the specimen → +10 Credibility to the Mole (always, win or lose).
      • Mole caught → +10 Credibility to each Field Researcher and Lead Biologist.
      • Discredited clue (Peer Review) → −5 Credibility to the author.

      Credibility accumulates across all habitats. Highest total wins the expedition.

      All observations and Credibility will be lost.

      Operations Manual ⚓

      How to run a successful deployment.

      The Grid

      A 5×5 grid of 25 words is dealt face-down. Words belong to one of four roles: Friendly Payloads (9 for the first team, 8 for the second), Enemy Payloads, Spiked Urchins (4 hazard tiles), and Mines (4 hazard tiles). Only the Captain can see the roles.

      The Captain

      Each team's Captain sees the colour-coded grid and gives the Crew a Sonar Ping — one word + one number. The word hints at multiple targets. The number says how many payloads it covers. Pass the phone to your Crew.

      The Crew

      Using the Sonar Ping, the Crew taps grid words in the order they want to reveal them, then launches the sequence. You can guess up to the Captain's number + 1. You can also stop early.

      Hazard Outcomes

      • ✅ Friendly Payload — +10 Valour, keep going
      • ⚠️ Enemy Payload — enemy gains +10 Valour
      • 🦔 Spiked Urchin — −5 Valour
      • 💣 Pressure Mine — −20 Valour
      • 💀 Nuclear Mine — −1000 Valour, game over immediately

      Whether hazards end your turn is controlled in Settings.

      Winning

      The game ends when a team arms all of their payloads. The team with the highest Valour wins — not necessarily the team that finished first.
